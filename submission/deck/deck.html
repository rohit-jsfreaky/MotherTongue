Why it hurts

A proper version per region costs weeks. A cheap one sounds translated — and nobody trusts it unchecked.

Agencies + voice artists

A new script, a new voice and a new edit for every language — weeks of back and forth per campaign.

$2–20 per minute

What AI video translation costs today — and it still swaps words, not the way a city talks.

One wrong price

A wrong price or an invented offer in one language is a real loss — so every AI version gets checked by hand.

Why now

Open models can now see an ad, write and speak many languages, and check the result. Cheaply enough to make one version per audience.

MotherTongue runs every AI step on AMD: a version takes about ten model calls and two minutes — not an agency and a fortnight.
